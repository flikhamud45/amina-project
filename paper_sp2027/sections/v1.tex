\section{V1: Sending Int8 Values Instead of Claims}
\label{sec:v1}

At long prompts the proof consists mostly of claims, at 16--19 bits each after compression: about
6.6\,GB for Llama-2-7B at 2{,}048 tokens, close to its int8 weights. V1 sends instead the int8
value that the next operation reads. It costs prover time, so it is an option next to the protocol
of Section~\ref{sec:defence} (v0).

\subsection{Values and Windows}

In a decoder, every weight operation other than the embeddings and the LM head is read by exactly
one operation without weights. The query, key, value, gate and up projections (fc1 in OPT) feed a
requantisation $a = \Vrq_\ell(Z_\ell)$, with $\Vrq_\ell(z) = \mathrm{clamp}(\Vshift_\ell(z), -127,
127)$, $\Vshift_\ell(z) = \lfloor (m_\ell z + 2^{29})/2^{30} \rfloor$ and a public integer
$m_\ell$. The attention output and down projections (fc2) feed a residual addition of $\Delta =
\Vshift_\ell(Z_\ell)$, which clamps only the sum. V1 sends $s_\ell = a_\ell$ or $\Delta_\ell$
instead of $Z_\ell$. The verifier still recomputes every operation without weights ((S1) in
Section~\ref{sec:protocol}) and so knows each input $X_\ell$; it must be convinced that the hidden
$Z_\ell = A_\ell \hat X_\ell$, with $\hat X_\ell = [X_\ell; \mathbf 1]$, maps to $s_\ell$.

Let $\Vlo_\ell(v) = \lceil (v\,2^{30} - 2^{29})/m_\ell \rceil$, the smallest $z$ with
$\Vshift_\ell(z) = v$, and $W_\ell(v) = \Vlo_\ell(v+1) - \Vlo_\ell(v)$. Then
\begin{equation}
  \Vshift_\ell(z) = v \iff 0 \le z - \Vlo_\ell(v) < W_\ell(v),
  \label{eq:v1-window}
\end{equation}
and $W_\ell(v)$ is $\lfloor 2^{30}/m_\ell \rfloor$ or $\lceil 2^{30}/m_\ell \rceil$. A clamped
entry of a requantisation, with $a = \pm 127$ and $Z$ outside the window of $a$, is listed as an
\emph{exception} with its exact pre-activation $z$; the verifier checks $|z| < 2^{29}$ and
$\Vrq_\ell(z) = a$ and gives the entry the window $[z, z+1)$.

For entry $(i,j)$ of a \emph{cut} operation, one whose claims V1 replaces, let $L_{ij}$ be
$\Vlo_\ell(s_{ij})$, or $z$ for an exception, and $W_{ij}$ its width; both are public.
By~\eqref{eq:v1-window}, every sent value is correct if each entry of the residue
\begin{equation}
  \delta_\ell = A_\ell \hat X_\ell - L_\ell
  \label{eq:v1-residue}
\end{equation}
lies in its window $[0, W_{ij})$, which holds for the honest prover. Since $\delta_\ell$ is affine
in the committed weights, V1 reduces to a range check on a linear image of $A_\ell$.

\subsection{A Windowed LogUp with Integer Multiplicities}

We prove all range memberships at once with a logUp lookup argument~\cite{haboeck2022logup}
over the field $\Fext = \F[\xi]/(\xi^8 - 11)$, as Fiat--Shamir (Section~\ref{sec:fs}) needs degree
at least 7. Entry $(i,j)$ becomes the leaf $v_{ij} = \delta_{ij} + \xi\,W_{ij}$, and the table is
$\Vtab = \{ t + \xi w : w \in \mathcal W, 0 \le t < w \}$, where $\mathcal W$ holds the width 1 and
the two widths of each cut operation. As $1$ and $\xi$ are linearly independent over $\F$, a leaf
lies in $\Vtab$ exactly when $\delta_{ij} \bmod p \in [0, W_{ij})$, so one table serves every
width. The prover sends a multiplicity $\mu_\tau$ for each $\tau \in \Vtab$, and the verifier checks
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{i} \frac{1}{\alpha - v_i} \;=\; \sum_{\tau \in \Vtab} \frac{\mu_\tau}{\alpha - \tau}
  \label{eq:v1-logup}
\end{equation}
at a random $\alpha$, the left sum running over the $\Nleaf = \sum_{\ell \in \Vcut} N_\ell T$
leaves of the cut set $\Vcut$. The usual logUp lemma needs fewer leaves than the characteristic,
but our proofs hold up to $4.3 \cdot 10^9$ leaves, more than $p \approx 2.0 \cdot 10^9$, and $p$
equal leaves cancel. We therefore check two integer conditions.

\begin{lemma}[integer multiplicities]
  \label{lem:v1-logup}
  Let $v_1, \dots, v_N \in \Fext$ and let $\mu : \Vtab \to \{0, \dots, p-1\}$ satisfy
  $\sum_\tau \mu_\tau = N$ as integers. If $\sum_i 1/(X - v_i) = \sum_\tau \mu_\tau/(X - \tau)$
  in $\Fext(X)$, then every $v_i$ lies in $\Vtab$.
\end{lemma}

By unique partial fractions, leaf counts agree with the multiplicities modulo $p$ on the table and
are multiples of $p$ off it; the two conditions, each necessary, force the latter to zero
(Appendix~\ref{app:v1}). Rule (P5) below keeps honest multiplicities below $2^{29}$.

\subsection{Reducing the Lookup to the Weight Commitment}

A fractional-sum GKR protocol~\cite{gkr,papini2023logupgkr} reduces the left side
of~\eqref{eq:v1-logup} to one evaluation. The cut operations are packed into instances: matrices
of their stacked rows by the $T$ positions, padded to powers of two. A binary tree adds the
fractions $1/(\alpha - v)$ at real positions and $0/1$ at padding up to a root $\mathsf n_0 /
\mathsf d_0$, and each layer is reduced to the next by a sum-check~\cite{lfkn1992} of degree 3,
ending at a random point $\rho = (r_{\mathrm{col}}, r_{\mathrm{row}})$ with claims $\hat{\mathsf
n}, \hat{\mathsf d}$ on the leaf layer's multilinear extensions.

Let $e \in \Fext^{T}$ hold $\Veq(r_{\mathrm{col}}, j)$ for $j < T$, let $\chi_\ell \in
\Fext^{N_\ell}$ hold $\Veq(r_{\mathrm{row}}, o_\ell + i)$ for the rows $i$ of operation $\ell$ at
row offset $o_\ell$, and let $I(\rho)$, computable in time $O(n)$, be the multilinear extension of
the indicator of the real positions. The true values are
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    &\tilde{\mathsf n}(\rho) = I(\rho), \quad
     \tilde{\mathsf d}(\rho) = \alpha I(\rho) + 1 - I(\rho) - V(\rho), \\
    &V(\rho) = \textstyle\sum_{\ell} \chi_\ell^\T \big(A_\ell \hat X_\ell - L_\ell + \xi\, W_\ell\big)\, e ,
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:v1-final}
\end{equation}
summed over the instance's operations. The verifier computes every term except $(\chi_\ell^\T
A_\ell)(\hat X_\ell e)$, whose first factor is a folded row as in~\eqref{eq:freivalds} over
$\Fext$. The prover sends $u_\ell = \chi_\ell^\T A_\ell$, the verifier multiplies it by its own
$\bar x_\ell = \hat X_\ell e$, and the existing column check tests $u_\ell$ against $C_M$ as eight
base-field folded rows instead of $r$. That check needs only that $u_\ell$ precedes the columns,
not that $\chi_\ell$ is uniform. In $\Kpre$, whose secret vectors cannot anticipate $\chi_\ell$, the
prover sends $y_\ell = A_\ell \bar x_\ell$, checked as $\chi_s^\T y_\ell = u_s \bar x_\ell$ for each
secret vector $\chi_s$ and its folded row $u_s$. Unlike the lookups of zkLLM and
DeepProve~\cite{zkllm,deepprove}, V1 thus adds no commitment: it looks up values affine in committed
weights.

\subsection{Protocol and Soundness}

\para{Cut set.} The cut set $\Vcut$ is a public function of the graph, the commitment plan and $T$,
absorbed into the statement. An operation is cut if (P1)~its output is read only by a residual
addition or by a requantisation whose clamp lies within $[-128, 127]$; (P2)~it spans all $T$
positions; (P3)~its window widths lie in $[2, 2^{16}]$; (P4)~its honest pre-activations are below
$2^{29}$ in magnitude; and (P5)~each width is shared by fewer than $2^{29}$ entries. A transposed
matrix is cut with all its members or not at all. The other weight operations form the clear set
$\Vclr$ and keep v0: the embeddings, the LM head, the single-position operations of the pruned last
block, and any operation that fails a rule.

\para{One query.} The prover sends the values $s_\ell$, the clear claims, the exceptions and the
multiplicities before $\alpha \in \Fext \setminus (\F + \xi\F)$ is drawn, then the GKR
transcripts, then the folds; the columns are drawn last. The verifier checks~\eqref{eq:v1-logup} as
$\sum_\gamma \mathsf n_0^\gamma / \mathsf d_0^\gamma = \sum_\tau \mu_\tau / (\alpha - \tau)$ over
the instances $\gamma$ (all checks: Appendix~\ref{app:v1}).

\begin{theorem}[soundness of V1]
  \label{thm:v1}
  Suppose that $C_M$ is computed honestly from the weights of $M$ and that the prover finds no hash
  collision. For every input $x$ and every prover, the V1 verifier of mode $\sC$ accepts an output
  $y \neq M(x)$ (in a generation query, an output that violates the token rule) with probability
  at most
  \[
    \begin{aligned}
      \varepsilon^{\mathrm{V1}}_{\sC} = {} & \frac{\Nleaf + |\Vtab|}{p^8 - p^2}
        + \sum_{\gamma} \varepsilon_{\mathrm{GKR}}(n_\gamma) \\
      & + \sum_{j \in \mathcal J} \prod_{i < t_j} \frac{k_j - 1 - i}{n_j - i}
        + \sum_{j \in \mathcal J_{\Vclr}} p^{-r},
    \end{aligned}
  \]
  where $\gamma$ ranges over the GKR instances, $n_\gamma$ is the number of variables of
  $\gamma$, $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{GKR}}(n) = \big(1 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} (3k+2)\big)/p^8$, $\mathcal
  J$, $k_j$, $n_j$ and $t_j$ are as in Theorem~\ref{thm:sound}, and $\mathcal J_{\Vclr} \subseteq
  \mathcal J$ holds the matrices of clear operations. In mode $\Kpre$ the last two sums are replaced
  by $\sum_\ell p^{-r}$ over the weight operations that are not looked up, and over $Q$ queries
  that reuse the secret vectors a false acceptance occurs with probability at most $Q$ times this
  bound.
\end{theorem}
% The Freivalds term runs over all clear committed matrices (as in thm:sound) and the column
% term takes thm:sound's product form, at most ((k_j - 1)/n_j)^{t_j}; V1_SPEC.md writes both
% more loosely.

\noindent\emph{Proof sketch} (Appendix~\ref{app:v1}). Let $\ell^\ast$ be the first wrong
operation; its input is honest. A clear $\ell^\ast$ is caught as in Theorem~\ref{thm:sound}. A cut
$\ell^\ast$ has, by (P4) and the range checks, a leaf outside $\Vtab$, so by
Lemma~\ref{lem:v1-logup} the two sides of~\eqref{eq:v1-logup} differ at $\alpha$ except with the
first term. GKR carries the wrong root to the final claims, whose check fails unless a fold is
wrong, which the column check or the secret vectors catch. \qed

\smallskip
At $\lambda = 128$, for Llama-2-13B at 2{,}048 tokens, the first term is at most $2^{-215.3}$ and
the GKR terms are $2^{-230.7}$ (Table~\ref{tab:params}); they change neither $r$ nor the column
counts. Each new Fiat--Shamir round errs with probability at most $2^{-215.3}$, below the per-round
error of Section~\ref{sec:fs}.

\para{Implementation and cost.} V1 is implemented for modes $\sC$ and $\Kpre$, with a GKR prover in
Triton~\cite{tillet2019triton}. Its tests reject tampered values, exceptions, multiplicities, GKR
and fold messages. V1's proof is 2.0--2.3$\times$ smaller than v0's at 2{,}048 tokens with random
weights and 1.7--1.8$\times$ on the real OPT-125M at 512 tokens, for about 10$\times$ the prover
time (Section~\ref{sec:eval-v1}) \todo{L40S: full models}.
